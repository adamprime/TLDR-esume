## 🧩 Signature Skill Stacks

### 🎥 From CLI to Clarity  
**Stack:** Screencasting + CLI support + customer empathy  
**Problem:** A powerful “point-in-time recovery” feature was hidden behind a CLI interface—confusing for non-technical users.  
**Solution:** I produced a video-based training course that introduced CLI basics, explained workflows, and offered real troubleshooting strategies.  
**Impact:** Enabled non-technical customers to self-serve, reducing ticket volume and increasing adoption of advanced product features.

---

### 🧠 Rebuilding the Internal Wiki with Loop  
**Stack:** Tool integration + knowledge management + UX thinking  
**Problem:** After moving fully into the Microsoft ecosystem, our team lost access to Confluence-style documentation workflows.  
**Solution:** I designed and implemented a system using Microsoft Loop + Teams, creating a seamless knowledge-sharing experience with SSO, embeds, and templates.  
**Impact:** Improved internal knowledge access, increased cross-functional collaboration, and reestablished documentation culture.

---

### 🛍️ QR-Driven Swag Fulfillment for Events  
**Stack:** E-commerce + API integrations + graphic design + event ops  
**Problem:** The sales team couldn’t bring physical swag to every conference.  
**Solution:** I built a QR-based swag redemption workflow: attendees scan, choose products, and get them shipped via automated fulfillment.  
**Impact:** Made our team more agile across events, cut shipping overhead, and personalized user experience.
