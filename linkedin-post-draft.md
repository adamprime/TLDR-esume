# LinkedIn Post Draft - TLDR;esume Launch

Job hunting is brutal right now. Layoffs everywhere, hundreds of applicants per role, and the endless grind of customizing resumes for each application.

I've been thinking about this problem and built something that might help: **TLDR;esume** — a free, open-source tool that runs on your local machine.

**What it does:**
- Gives you an honest "fit assessment" before you waste time applying to roles that aren't a good fit
- Tailors your resume for each job using AI — but *only* facts from your actual resume (no hallucinated accomplishments)
- Generates cover letters with personality, not corporate drone speak
- Exports clean PDFs in multiple styles
- Works with Claude or OpenAI — your choice, your API key, your data stays local

**What it doesn't do:**
- Cost you a monthly subscription
- Send your resume to some startup's servers
- Make up fake metrics to make you sound impressive (Yes, increasing revenue 2400% year over year looks nice, but only if it's true)

It's yours to fork, use, and modify however you want: https://github.com/adamprime/TLDR-esume

If you know someone who's currently in the job market, feel free to share. And if you try it out, I'd love to hear what you think. No strings attached. I hope it's helpful if you're in the job market now.
