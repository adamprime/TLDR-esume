About the Builder

TLDR;esume was built by Adam Tervort during an active job search. The problem was personal: copying resumes into ChatGPT for every application was tedious, error-prone, and slow. So he built a tool to fix it.

That's what Adam does. He builds the first version of things.

(He also sometimes talks about himself in the third person, but we'll forgive that quirk for the moment.)

0 to 1 Builder

Adam is a generalist who turns ambiguous problems into working systems. Over 11 years, he's led operations, engineering, support, and HR at a cybersecurity startup—helping it grow from a scrappy team into a global SaaS company.

But the through-line has always been building:

  • • TLDR;esume — AI-powered resume tailoring (you're using it)
  • • Alignment Engines — Production SaaS for AI-powered coaching (alignmentengines.com)
  • • ComicCaster — RSS feeds for 500+ webcomics (comiccaster.xyz)
  • • Bishopric OS — Notion-based operating system for volunteer leadership
  • • Internal tools, automations, and workflows — Too many to list

Adam builds with whatever gets the job done: Next.js, Python, SvelteKit, FastAPI, single-file HTML tools, Notion databases, AI integrations. The tech doesn't matter as much as shipping something useful.
